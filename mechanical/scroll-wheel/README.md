# Scroll-wheel assembly

Reserve editable wheel/shaft/mount CAD here. Integrate the encoder-free GB1806,
bearings and a dedicated diametric MA735 magnet. Define air gap, concentricity,
shaft retention, clearance and middle-button load path. Verify rotor and button
magnetic fields do not corrupt angle sensing. Motor dimensions and pole-pair
count from the notes require confirmation against the purchased variant.

The selected architecture uses a [small upright encoder board](ENCODER_BOARD.md)
for U27/C62, facing a shaft-end magnet. The motherboard is provisionally
60 x 95 mm. The [14 x 18 mm encoder PCB and JST harness](../../hardware/encoder/README.md)
are implemented; mounting, cable fit and full wheel fit remain to be verified.
