# ACT_5V regenerative-brake simulation

This is an isolated circuit-model study. It does not change the production schematic, PCB, panel or manufacturing package. The completed PCB baseline is commit `145d2be`.

Read [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) for the measured results and [the plots](results/waveforms.png). `results/metrics.csv` provides the comparison table. Full waveforms, per-run decks and routine logs are retained locally and regenerated by `run.py`; they are ignored by Git. Compact reports, plots, source inventories and the failed-model diagnostic log are versioned. `results/file-sha256.json` records both versioned files and locally retained run artifacts, so a fresh checkout intentionally does not contain every listed file.

## Circuit and sources

`run.py` derives resistor values, connections, bleeders and directly connected ACT_5V capacitors from the Main/Wheel/Encoder XML netlists. The inventories and source hashes are in `results/inventory.json`. Current nominal capacitance is **210.7 uF**, including C90; the separate **47 uF effective** case intentionally gives much less energy storage. All this capacitance is lumped at one rail node. Charge-pump and other non-ACT-to-ground capacitors are excluded.

- U30: [TI TLV1811 PSpice model](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/snom762), original file retained locally. Its first switching event failed ngspice convergence at approximately 1.11814 ms. The diagnostic log is versioned in `model-checks/`; the original deck remains local because it embeds TI model text. `run.py --vendor-reproducer` generates an equivalent deck for further investigation. It is **not** used in the accepted sweep.
- Comparator fallback: continuous high-gain decision with 0.1 mV smoothing, 0.5 mV nominal offset, transport delays of 0.45/5/10 us and 60 ohm output resistance. The external divider, 3 MOhm feedback chain, C67 and gate network retain their actual netlist connections. This is an engineering approximation, not a validated TLV1811 macro-model. The 0.45 us delay and 60 ohm drive come from TI's model; 5/10 us are deliberate stresses, not claimed datasheet limits.
- U31: [TI LM4040_NA2P5 unencrypted model](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/snom526), used unchanged through ngspice's `psa` compatibility mode. The two tolerance cases select its `TOL=+1/-1` parameter. A vendor model does not establish full production tolerance coverage.
- Q5: no manufacturer SPICE model was located on the [AO3400A product page](https://www.aosmd.com/products/mosfets/low-voltage-mosfets-12v-30v/ao3400a). A switch approximates 32 mOhm on-resistance and an equivalent gate capacitance of 6 nC / 4.5 V = 1.333 nF. It switches on at 2.55 V and off at 2.45 V; this small numerical hysteresis is not an actual MOSFET parameter. The approximation omits the Miller plateau, body diode and temperature dependence. The 32 mOhm rating applies at 4.5 V drive, not throughout gate charging. Stress cases use 64 mOhm; the combined corners also use 2 nF and 120 ohm driver resistance.
- Threshold stresses select upper/lower divider corners at +/-1%, reference-model tolerance selection, and comparator offset +/-4 mV. The comparator offset bounds are from the [TI data sheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv1811.pdf); resistor percentages and doubled gate/drive/on-resistance values are selected engineering stresses. Brake resistor stress is +5%. These are selected corners, not a statistical or full-temperature qualification.

Source URLs, model hashes, engine hash and production-file preservation checks are in `results/validation.json`. The engine used is **ngspice 46 from KiCad 10.0**, with its installed code-model modules. Downloaded TI models retain their copyright and disclaimer headers. Their redistribution rights have not been established, so the models and decks embedding them stay out of Git under the repository's contribution policy. `fetch_models.py` downloads them from TI and refuses a revision whose model SHA-256 differs from this study.

## Operating assumptions

- Start from a settled, approximately 5 V operating point; cold startup and comparator power-on-reset are not modeled. Reference startup below regulation is not accepted by this study.
- Regeneration is an injected current, not an electrical/mechanical motor model. No MCU or FOC firmware is simulated.
- The upstream source supplies current through an assumed 0.15 ohm effective source resistance, but cannot absorb current. Unplug switches it off. This represents the reverse-blocking boundary, not an eFuse transistor model.
- All rail capacitance is lumped. PCB/cable inductance, capacitor ESR/ESL, local-versus-remote capacitor access, switch-node ringing and TVS behavior are omitted. The 47 uF case is a capacitance stress, not a substitute for modeling those parasitics.
- Other active loads are omitted; netlist bleeders, the reference and the brake network remain. The behavioral comparator is not a power-conserving transistor model: its ideal control/drive sources do not fully charge their energy to ACT_5V. Gate-drive energy is small in this study but is not a validated loss estimate.
- No credit is taken for the TVS. Diagnostic excursions above 6.5 V indicate failed brake absorption in this model; their amplitudes do not predict the real TVS-clamped rail.

## Cases and acceptance

24 cases run at **200 ns and 100 ns maximum timesteps**: two capacitances, three delays, single/repeated/unplug pulses, brake-disabled and 1 A controls, on-resistance stress, two combined threshold/drive corners, and a slow rail sweep.

The repeated case injects 0.4 A for 1 ms every 6 ms, with 1 us edges; its integrated average regenerative power must remain below 0.5 W. The single pulse is a burst test, not continuous 0.5 W operation. Unplug occurs at 1.05 ms, during regeneration. The brake should continue without upstream power; this does not simulate connector inductive interruption.

The voltage target is below 6.5 V for intended-current cases. Response is measured from the comparator input crossing its **offset-adjusted** reference to the brake bank reaching 0.4 A. It excludes sense-filter delay before that crossing and does not equal a complete physical fault-to-clamp time. Failures of the 10 us screen remain visible; numerical success must not turn them into passes.

Numerical acceptance requires all transients to finish, finite vectors, peak-voltage change below 5 mV with timestep halving, response change below 0.5 us and brake-energy change below 1%. The rail-boundary balance compares integrated input energy with integrated load-port energy plus capacitor stored-energy change, within 0.5%. It is a KCL/integration check on the modeled rail boundary, not validation of all internal macro-model energy sources. The slow-ramp thresholds must be within 20 mV of the ideal resistor calculation. Both deliberate fault controls must violate the rail target.

No temperature, resistor pulse-rating, MOSFET SOA, USB, ADC noise or physical braking acceptance follows from these results. No component or PCB change is made based on the model alone.

## Reproduce

Use 64-bit Python 3.12 with NumPy and Matplotlib, plus KiCad 10.0's `ngspice.dll` and sibling `lib/ngspice/*.cm` modules. Override the engine location with `--dll` if necessary. Requirements used for reviewing the results are listed in `requirements.txt`.

```powershell
python -m pip install -r hardware/simulation/brake/requirements.txt
python hardware/simulation/brake/fetch_models.py
python hardware/simulation/brake/run.py
python hardware/simulation/brake/review.py
```

For this workspace, plotting packages were installed only in ignored `build/simulation-python`. Set `$env:PYTHONPATH='E:/Projects/MagMouse/build/simulation-python'` when using that dependency directory. No global Python installation or KiCad preference was changed.

`--smoke` runs one case at one timestep; it does not meet full acceptance. `--only TEXT` selects cases for debugging. These overwrite the summary with the selected subset; run the full command before `review.py`. The review refuses incomplete case suites, recomputes metrics from saved waveforms and compares production files byte-for-byte with commit `145d2be`. The original vendor-model failed circuit requires `psa` compatibility if investigated independently.

`fetch_models.py --check` verifies local models without network access. No simulator or code-model binary is distributed in this repository. Supporting Python scripts are GPL-3.0-or-later under the repository's split-license policy; that does not relicense TI's downloads.
