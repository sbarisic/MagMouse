## Datasheet and pin review

Manufacturer PDFs were inspected locally, including package drawings; vendor
PDFs remain in ignored build output. Sources below identify the reviewed data.

- [TUSB320LAI](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tusb320lai.pdf), SLLSEQ8D,
  Rev. D, May 2017: 1/2 CC1/CC2, 3 PORT, 4 VBUS_DET, 5 ADDR, 6 OUT3,
  7/8 OUT1/OUT2, 9 ID, 10 GND, 11 EN_N, 12 VDD. ADDR is intentionally NC;
  PORT and EN_N are grounded. No external Rd. Pull-ups share VDD.
- [TPS25947](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf), SLVSFC9C,
  Rev. C, May 2026: 1 EN/UVLO, 2 OVLO, 3 AUXOFF, 4 FLT, 5 IN, 6 OUT,
  7 dVdt, 8 GND, 9 ILM, 10 ITIMER. The selected 470L has active limiting
  and thermal latch-off. Both ITIMER pins and unused AUXOFF outputs are NC.
- [TPS6216x](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62160.pdf), SLVSAM2E,
  Rev. E, May 2017: 1 PGND, 2 VIN, 3 EN, 4 AGND, 5 FB, 6 VOS, 7 SW,
  8 PG, 9 exposed pad. Fixed-output 62162 grounds FB; VOS senses +3V3.
  The former TLV62569/R1/R2 circuit is retired, not fitted in parallel.
- [Sunlord SWPA](https://atta.szlcsc.com/upload/public/pdf/source/20210916/AC798BA7E846B7D0B6818F45CD84DB0B.pdf),
  2021/05/15: the 3.3 uH SWPA4020S row has 0.091 ohm maximum DCR,
  3.20 A tabulated saturation current and 1.40 A heat-rating current.
  Ratings use 30% inductance reduction and 40 C temperature rise respectively.
  This supports a starting 1 A logic design, not a board thermal guarantee.
- [SN74LVC1G123](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g123.pdf),
  SCES586E, Rev. E, March 2024: 1 A, 2 B, 3 CLR, 4 GND, 5 Q,
  6 Cext, 7 Rext/Cext, 8 VCC.
  C27 goes between pins 6/7, not from a timing pin to ground. A single-unit
  local symbol includes the power pins explicitly.
- [SN74LVC08A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc08a.pdf)
  (SCAS283W, July 2024) and
  [SN74LVC2G04](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g04.pdf)
  (SCES195N, August 2015): quad
  AND gates use 1/2→3, 4/5→6, 9/10→8, 12/13→11, power 14/7.
  Inverter uses 1→6, 3→4, power 5/2. Unused AND inputs are grounded.
- [TLV9001](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9001.pdf), SBOS833R,
  November 2021: DBV pins
  1 OUT, 2 V-, 3 IN+, 4 IN-, 5 V+. IN- connects to OUT for unity gain.
- [TPD2E2U06](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tpd2e2u06.pdf), SLLSEG9C,
  December 2019:
  DCK pins 1/2 are signal channels; pin 3 is ground. DRL pin numbers differ.
- [JSCJ 2N7002](https://www.jscj-elec.com/gallery/file/2N7002%20SOT-23%20V2.0.pdf),
  Rev. 2.0: 1 gate, 2 source, 3 drain. Its selected branch currents are small;
  do not interpret a threshold voltage as a guaranteed power-switch on-resistance.
- [Diodes BAT54](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/BAT54.pdf),
  DS11005 Rev. 34-2, November 2023: anode 1, cathode 3, pad 2 NC.
  D3 cathode goes to ACT_DRIVE_EN; D4 cathode goes to +3V3.
- [Littelfuse SMBJ series](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/4ab84a227fae39f9eac85241b8264ead.pdf?productCode=C83270),
  revision JC.07/04/25 v4: check the exact 12 A and 6.0 A part rows,
  polarity, transient derating, footprint and specified copper area.

## Footprints and provenance

U11 uses KiCad's Texas_X2QFN-12_1.6x1.6mm_P0.4mm; U2 uses
Texas_DSG0008A_WSON-8-1EP_2x2mm_P0.5mm_EP0.9x1.6mm. U15 uses
VSSOP-8_2.3x2mm_P0.5mm and the AND gates use TSSOP-14_4.4x5mm_P0.65mm.
Do not exchange package suffixes without remapping pins and lands.

The new [Texas_RPW0010A footprint](MagMouse.pretty/Texas_RPW0010A.kicad_mod)
is registered through [fp-lib-table](fp-lib-table). It is original geometry
drawn from TI's RPW0010A land/stencil example, drawing 4225183/A, 08/2019
(reviewed datasheet pages 73/74). Key checks:

- Body 2 x 2 mm; central power pads 5/6 at x ±0.25 mm, size 0.30 x 2.40 mm.
- Side pads at x ±0.90 mm, y ±0.225/±0.70 mm. Corner pads use overlapping
  lands carrying the same pin number, with the vertical segment centered at
  x ±0.725 mm / y ±0.875 mm. These are ten electrical pins, not fourteen.
- Central paste apertures are 0.28 x 1.06 mm at y ±0.63 mm; corner paste
  follows the reduced TI example. No extra exposed ground pad is invented.
- Copper gap between power pads is 0.20 mm. Confirm solder-mask webs,
  paste merging, stencil thickness, copper thermal spreading and JLC assembly
  inspection with the actual board. A missing 3D model does not replace the
  package drawing for mechanical clearance.

New IC symbols are original pin-map drawings. BAT54_7_F derives from KiCad's
Device:D_Schottky with pad numbering changed to 3/1. Preserve the attribution
and license in [KICAD-LIBRARY-LICENSE.md](KICAD-LIBRARY-LICENSE.md).
The symbol library uses CC-BY-SA-4.0 with KiCad exception; the original footprint
and electronic design use the repository's CERN-OHL-S-2.0 license.

## Reproducible checks

Run export_review.py followed by verify_power.py from the repository root.
The first generates the current sheets, netlist, ERC and review BOM. The second
checks that no sheet components disappeared during export, checks symbol pins
against footprint pads, verifies critical connections and evaluates 4,096
combinations of source, reset, arm, sensor request, permit and six commands.
It also checks the programmed current-limit margin. Reports are written to
build/kicad-review. These checks do not simulate analog startup, watchdog RC
corners, USB protocol, capacitor bias, thermal behavior or regenerative energy.
